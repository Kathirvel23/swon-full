The Problem

Temporary work today runs on
trust that isn't there.

For Workers

  • !Jobs found through word of mouth — hard to know which are genuine
  • !Pay agreed verbally, with no record when something goes wrong
  • !No proof of past work to show the next host

For Hosts

  • !Urgent need for people, with no reliable way to find them
  • !Last-minute no-shows disrupt the whole day
  • !Attendance tracked on paper, payments kept by memory
SWON's answer One structured journey — from finding work to getting paid — where both sides can see and trust every step.

How SWON Works

One journey.
Eight clear steps.

01

Discover

Browse nearby opportunities that fit your skills and time.

02

Pairing

See why you match — skills, distance and trust, made visible.

03

Flight Request

The worker raises a request; the host reviews it in one place.

04

Bond

The host hires — both sides commit to the same clear terms.

05

Formation

Teams are organized — who works where, on which day.

06

Attendance

Check-in and check-out are recorded live at the site.

07

Earnings

Work is approved and pay is recorded — transparently.

08

TrustScore

Every completed journey builds a reputation that opens doors.
